## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported |
|---|---|
| Latest release | Yes |
| Previous minor | Security fixes only |
| Older | No |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report security issues privately. Do not open a public issue.

- Email: security@YOUR-DOMAIN.com
- GitHub: use **Security > Report a vulnerability** on this repository (private advisory).

Include: affected URL or component, steps to reproduce, impact, and any proof of concept.

## What to Expect

| Step | Target time |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | 2 business days |
| Initial assessment | 5 business days |
| Fix for Critical / High | 7 to 30 days |
| Public disclosure | After a fix is released, coordinated with you |

## Safe Harbor

We will not take legal action against good-faith research that avoids privacy violations, data destruction, service disruption, and social engineering, and that gives us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosure.

## Out of Scope

- Denial of service and volumetric attacks
- Social engineering of staff or customers
- Findings from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact
- Missing best-practice headers without an exploitable issue
